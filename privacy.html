Brain Blast Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Brain Blast is a trivia game. It runs as a web page, as a YouTube Playable, as a Reddit app and as an Android app. This page explains what each version collects. The short version: the game itself has no accounts and keeps your progress on your device or with the platform you play on. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects data to serve them.

What the game stores

Your progress: best scores, which questions you have seen, your Daily Challenge streak, your language and sound settings. Where it is kept depends on where you play:

  • Web and Android app: only on your device (the browser's or app's local storage). It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.
  • YouTube: in YouTube's cloud save for the game, under your YouTube account, so it follows you between devices. YouTube is also sent your Daily Challenge score. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Reddit: on the app's storage at Reddit, linked to your Reddit account: your progress, your Reddit username, your Daily Challenge and Block Blitz scores, and how long you took to answer (used to rank ties and to spot cheating). Your username and scores appear on the subreddit's leaderboards. Any player can remove all of this with "Brain Blast: delete my data" in the subreddit menu. Reddit's privacy policy applies.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows full-screen ads between rounds, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads,
  • crash and performance diagnostics for the ads SDK.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and "Privacy options" in the game's language menu lets you change your choice at any time. Elsewhere, you can opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account to play.
  • We don't sell your data. Apart from the ads described above and the platforms you choose to play on, nothing about you is shared.
  • The web, YouTube and Reddit versions show no ads.

Children

Brain Blast is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Reddit version, contact us and we will delete it.

Contests

A subreddit may run a monthly prize for the top Daily Challenge player. If you win, the subreddit's moderators contact you through Reddit to arrange the prize; the contact details you give them are used only for that. Each contest's official rules are posted in the subreddit.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/xdsliperz1830-eng/TriviaMania/issues.
